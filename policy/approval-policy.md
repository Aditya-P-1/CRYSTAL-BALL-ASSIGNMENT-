# OomniEye Maintenance & Approval Policy

## 1. Scope
This document covers the standard operating procedures for reviewing and approving items in the OomniEye dashboard, specifically for Site Patrol, Drone Video demos, Safety Equipment, and Spatial Zone Layouts.

## 2. Priority & Urgency
- **Safety Equipment & Sensor Specs (PDFs):** These are always the highest priority. They must be reviewed immediately to ensure operational safety.
- **Drone Patrol Videos:** High priority. Must be reviewed within 24 hours of submission.
- **Spatial Zone Layouts (Images):** Medium priority.
- **Onboarding & Checklists (Folders):** Low priority, can be reviewed weekly.

## 3. Review Process
1. **Safety Equipment (PDF):** Open the PDF. Check that the sensor specifications match the local safety regulations. Approve if valid. If any details are missing, reject and request a revision.
2. **Drone Patrol (Video):** Watch the entire demo video. Ensure the drone completes its full autonomous path without obstruction. Approve if the flight path is safe.
3. **Spatial Zone Layouts:** Compare the camera map against the site's blind spots. Ensure full coverage.
4. **Onboarding Folders:** Ensure all checklist items are ticked off by the submitting admin.

## 4. General Rule
If any submission is older than 5 days, it must be escalated to the Shift Supervisor immediately rather than being processed normally.
